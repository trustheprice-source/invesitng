Keep a progress record, preserve failed experiments and stop only at a concrete blocker or verified completion of the authorized release scope. Do not search indefinitely until a strategy passes.

Do not purchase data or bypass restrictions. If access is blocked, implement/test an adapter against clearly synthetic fixtures and continue other ready work.

Do not require nominal buy/hold outperformance when exposure differs; explain the comparison.

A modern LLM's knowledge of later outcomes can contaminate historical sentiment tests. Use temporally valid extractors or prospective-only evaluation, not role-play dates.

Research replay is not prospective paper performance.

12. A live broker adapter and funded experiment are a separate future authorization and engineering milestone. The current project cannot place real orders.

## When blocked

Record the exact missing dataset/permission/definition, evidence of the failed route, useful work completed independently and the smallest user action that would unblock it. Never invent credentials, paid access, broker features or missing market observations. Preserve restart commands and hashes.
